# General Checklist — applies to every deliverable

- **(critical)** Confidence in correctness & completeness is `>= 80%`.
- **(critical)** Output matches the scope that was requested — nothing missing,
  nothing invented beyond scope.
- **(critical)** No unresolved `TBD`, `TODO`, `???`, or placeholder content in a
  deliverable presented as complete.
- Internally consistent — no contradictions within the document or with prior
  approved artifacts.
- All assumptions are stated explicitly, not buried or implied.
- Terminology is consistent with earlier artifacts (same names for same things).
- The deliverable is in the expected location and format for its phase.
- It is genuinely ready for the next persona to consume without rework.
- Complies with `CLAUDE.md` Sections 1 & 2 where code or technical specs are
  involved.
